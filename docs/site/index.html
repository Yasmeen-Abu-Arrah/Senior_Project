AI-Driven Arabic Speech Rehabilitation Platform

Senior Graduation Project — Arab American University (AAUP)

Author

Computer Systems Engineering

Published

July 9, 2026

Overview

Communication is a fundamental human right and a critical vector for cognitive, social, and emotional development. Pediatric communication disorders present unique developmental hurdles that, if left unaddressed, can lead to permanent academic and social marginalization.

This project integrates computer vision, speech signal processing, and generative AI to deliver an accessible, automated, and dialect-aware rehabilitation tool tailored for Palestinian children.

The Problem

In Palestine, pediatric communication disorders pose a severe public health crisis. Children suffering from disorders such as:

  • Childhood Apraxia of Speech (CAS)
  • Verbal stuttering
  • Phonological deletion
  • Environmental language delays

…face profound barriers to social integration.

The core issue lies in a compounding deficit of:

Barrier Description
Clinical Shortages Acute shortage of certified Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs)
Geographical Barriers Infrastructure centralized in urban centers; rural families face prohibitive transport costs
Dialectal Void AI tools built for Western languages or MSA — not Palestinian/Levantine dialect

Local Statistics (Palestine)

ImportantKey Data — Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics (PCBS)
  • Communication disabilities are the 2nd most prevalent childhood disability in Palestine — 37.3% of all pediatric disability cases
  • West Bank: 35.7% — Gaza Strip: 40.1%
  • Jenin Governorate (AAUP home region): highest disability rate in the West Bank at 4.1% of the total population
  • Rural children face an Odds Ratio (OR) of 4.4 risk of prolonged speech delays due to low income
  • Congenital factors account for 33.6% of communication disabilities

System Architecture

The platform is composed of 7 modules that address different aspects of speech rehabilitation:

Module Overview

Saif — Lip Vision

Real-time Childhood Apraxia of Speech (CAS) assessment using MediaPipe Face Mesh.
Calculates the Lip Pucker Ratio (LPR) to track jaw drops and lip-rounding.

View Details →

Layla — Speech Analysis

Arabic phonological analysis and fluency detection using OpenAI Whisper.
Targets phoneme-level error classification for stuttering and deletion disorders.

View Details →

Habibi TTS

Dialect-aware Arabic text-to-speech engine supporting MSA, Levantine, Egyptian, Saudi, and UAE dialects.

View Details →

Multimodal System

Combined vision + audio pipeline integrating lip tracking and speech scoring into a single unified Gradio interface.

View Details →

Story Generator

Offline Qwen LLM that generates child-safe, vocalized Arabic therapy stories with target words embedded.

View Details →

Maze Craze Game

Browser-based gamification layer — children navigate mazes to practice speech targets in a rewarding context.

View Details →

## System Architecture

The platform is composed of **7 modules** that address different aspects of speech rehabilitation:

```{mermaid}
graph TD
    A[AI Arabic Speech Rehabilitation Platform] --> B[Saif Module\nLip Apraxia Vision]
    A --> C[Layla Module\nSpeech-to-Text]
    A --> D[Habibi TTS\nArabic Synthesis]
    A --> E[Multimodal System\nCombined Pipeline]
    A --> F[Story Generator\nQwen LLM]
    A --> G[Qwen3 ASR\nAdvanced STT]
    A --> H[Maze Craze\nGamification]

    B --> B1[MediaPipe Face Mesh\nLPR Calculation]
    C --> C1[OpenAI Whisper\nArabic Phonology]
    D --> D1[Habibi-TTS\nDialect-Aware]
    F --> F1[Offline Qwen LLM\nArabic Stories]
    G --> G1[Qwen3-ASR\nWord-Level Timestamps]
```
